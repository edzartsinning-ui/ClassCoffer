Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and Mac with macOS 14 or later. The interface adapts to screen size and light or dark appearance.

Can I manage multiple classes?

Yes. Each class can have multiple accounts or activities. The total overview includes archived accounts.

Is the app connected to a bank account?

No. ClassCoffer records transactions but does not send payments or retrieve bank transactions.

How do I change language?

Use “Language” in the sidebar. Choose the system language or German, English, French, Spanish, Italian or Dutch. Your choice is saved.
