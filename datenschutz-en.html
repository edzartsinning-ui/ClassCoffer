Privacy

Updated: 27 October 2026

Data processing in the app

The app stores the classes, student names, accounts, transactions, notes and imported data you enter. It does not send this data to the developer and contains no analytics or advertising services.

Backups and sharing

You choose where to export PDF, CSV, ZIP or JSON files. Exports may contain personal data. The local working file has no additional app-password encryption. Encrypted JSON exports are optional.

This website

This site uses no external fonts, analytics or advertising. Only your language choice is stored locally in the browser. Once published, the hosting provider may process technical access data; information must be completed for the chosen host.

Contact and school responsibility

Contact Edzart Sinning at esinning@web.de. For school use, the responsible school authority determines the purpose and scope of processing. Direct requests about school records to that authority.
